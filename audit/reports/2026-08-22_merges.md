Rule (Andrea): datasets that are batches of ONE condition are merged by pooling ratings per item.

- Near-duplicates with the same instruction and item containment >= 0.90 (strict subset re-runs, or lists differing in a few items): merged by pooling on SHARED items; non-shared items are kept with fewer ratings. Connected components were merged transitively.
