For each word, participants were asked to enter the age (in years) at which they thought they had learned the word. By learning a word, “we mean the age at which you would have understood that word if somebody had used it in front of you, EVEN IF YOU DID NOT use, read, or write it at the time.”

Stimulus format:
<<{word}>>
What is the age (in years) at which you think you learned this word? Respond with the age in years as a number.
