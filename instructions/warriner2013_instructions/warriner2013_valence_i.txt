You are invited to take part in the study that is investigating emotion, and concerns how people respond to different types of words. You will use a scale to rate how you felt while reading each word. Please work at a rapid pace and don't spend too much time thinking about each word. Rather, make your ratings based on your first and immediate reaction as you read each word.

The data use a low-to-high valence scale from 1 (unhappy/negative) to 9 (happy/positive). At one extreme of this scale, you are unhappy, annoyed, unsatisfied, melancholic, despaired, or bored. At the other extreme, you are happy, pleased, satisfied, contented, or hopeful. If you feel completely neutral, neither happy nor sad, select the middle of the scale (rating 5).

Please rate the word <<{word}>> on a scale from 1 (unhappy/negative) to 9 (happy/positive). Answer with one digit.
