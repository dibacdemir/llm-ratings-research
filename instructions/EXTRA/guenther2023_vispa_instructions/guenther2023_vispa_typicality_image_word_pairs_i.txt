The exact instructions are not reproduced verbatim in the paper. The Procedure sections for each study paraphrase what participants were told. The verbatim instructions might be in the OSF repository at https://osf.io/qvw9c. What the paper reports:

Study 3 (Typicality ratings, image–word pairs): Participants were asked to "select the image that shows the least typical and the most typical exemplar of the word (i.e., the worst and the best representation of the word)." Further clarification stated participants could think of the task as choosing "for which image would the word presented to you be the worst and the best description," or "if you hear or read the word, which image is closest to the first image that comes to your mind?"

Please rate the image-word pair <<{image_word_pair}>> on a scale from 1 to 7. Answer with one digit.
