The exact instructions are not reproduced verbatim in the paper. The Procedure sections for each study paraphrase what participants were told. The verbatim instructions might be in the OSF repository at https://osf.io/qvw9c. What the paper reports:

Study 1 (Visual similarity ratings, word pairs): You are recruited for a study titled "How simila are these objects?." You have to indicate the degree of visual similarity between two objects, i.e., how much they look alike. For instance, ‘dog’ and ‘wolf’ have very high visual similarity, whereas ‘car’ and ‘street’ may be semantically similar but are not visually similar (a car does not look like a street).

Please rate the pair <<{pair}>> on a scale from 1 to 7. Answer with one digit.
